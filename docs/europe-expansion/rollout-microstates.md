# Microstate release checks

This group contains 12 reviewed destinations: Andorra 5, Liechtenstein 3, Monaco 1, San Marino 2 and Vatican City 1. Every destination has reviewed USGS earthquake monitoring, supplementary EMSC evidence and modeled weather/air quality. No microstate has an approved marine forecast cell. FCDO whole-country context covers the 11 destinations outside Vatican City; no Vatican foreign-advice target is invented.

Only Malbun has additional approved SLF avalanche monitoring. Its reviewed region and elevation contract must not be extended to Vaduz, Schaan, Andorra or an entire neighboring country. All other unapproved warning categories remain explicit gaps. Meteorological forecasts, seasonal bulletins and river measurements cannot substitute for warnings.

## Geography and official information

Liechtenstein links its own government natural-hazard information. SLF's verified Malbun applicability is independent of that general information link. San Marino retains its official civil-protection ordinance archive. Vatican City points to its official English information portal and inherits no Italian warning coverage.

Monaco links the official journal archive, verified on 2026-09-09 at [Journal de Monaco](https://journaldemonaco.gouv.mc/). The public link explicitly asks users to check dates and later updates.
