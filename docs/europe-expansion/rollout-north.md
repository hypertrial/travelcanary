## Country boundaries and linked information

The UK catalog covers the four nations and domestic islands. Environment Agency, SEPA, NRW and Northern Ireland information remain separately labeled. Road pointers identify England's National Highways, Scotland's trunk-road service, Traffic Wales and TrafficwatchNI. Powercut105 is an electricity operator directory for Great Britain; Northern Ireland has a separate NIE Networks outage link. These do not imply UK-wide transport or utility ingestion.

Norway excludes Svalbard and Jan Mayen. Varsom provides linked hazard information, and Statens vegvesen provides linked road information. Elvia outages apply only to its electricity service area and do not establish all-Norway utility coverage. Its official map may omit low-voltage faults.

Iceland links the Met Office, avalanche information and road conditions. Veitur outages apply only to its service networks; active, planned and completed notices must be distinguished on the official site. Neither road information nor aviation notices establish ground evacuation orders.

The country dossier retains exact unresolved gates for MeteoAlarm, NVE, national flood services, Iceland road ingestion and ground volcano information. Registration-gated routes make no requests. No neighboring-country or UK cross-nation warning inheritance is permitted.
